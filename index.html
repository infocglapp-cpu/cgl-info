Support & Help

CGL saves links from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and X into folders you organize yourself — synced through your own private iCloud account, with no server and no sign-in in between.

How to save

↑

Share Sheet

In TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X, Safari, or anywhere else, tap Share → CGL.

▦

Quick Save widget

Copy a link, then tap the CGL widget on your Home Screen — no share sheet needed.

⏻

Control Center

Add "Save to CGL" in Settings → Control Center for a one-tap save from the Lock Screen.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my saved content stored?

In your own private iCloud account — CGL doesn't run a server that holds your saved links, folders, or notes. That also means the same content shows up automatically on any of your devices signed into the same iCloud account.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login — your iCloud account is the only identity involved, and CGL never sees it directly.

How do I move a save into a different folder?

Open the item, tap the "Folder" row, and pick a folder — or Unsorted to file it back out. You can do this any time, not just right after saving.

How do I delete something?

Swipe left on any item in a list to delete it, or open a folder and use the trash icon in the top bar to delete everything in it at once.

Why doesn't a saved item have a title or thumbnail yet?

That detail is fetched in the background right after saving and usually appears within a few seconds. If it's still missing after reopening the app, it'll retry automatically the next time CGL comes to the foreground.

Does this work on iPad too?

Yes — install CGL on any of your devices signed into the same iCloud account and your saves, folders, and notes sync automatically.

Does CGL collect or sell my data?

No. There's no analytics, no ads, and nothing shared with third parties — the only place your saves exist is your own iCloud account.
